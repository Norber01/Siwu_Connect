# Google Play store listing — Siwu Connect

Copy these into Play Console → **Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing**.

## App name (max 30 characters)

Siwu Connect

## Short description (max 80 characters)

News, chat, sports and events for Akpafu's five towns — at home and abroad.

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Siwu Connect brings together the people of Akpafu Odomi, Akpafu Mempeasem, Akpafu Todzi, Akpafu Adokor and Akpafu Sokpo — wherever you live, in the towns, across Ghana or abroad. Five towns, one community.

📰 NEWS FROM HOME
• Follow your town's feed or the whole of Akpafu
• Post updates with photos, videos and polls
• React, comment, repost and share posts with friends
• Official announcements from town admins, and verified accounts with a blue tick

💬 CHAT
• Private chats with friends, with voice notes, photos, replies and reactions
• Group chats for family, classmates and associations
• A chat room for every town, and one for all of Akpafu
• See when friends are typing and when your message was seen

✨ STORIES
• Share moments that disappear after 24 hours — only your friends see them
• See who viewed your story

⚽ SPORTS CENTRE
• Live scores with a running match clock, fixtures, results and standings
• Predict the score before kick-off and climb the predictions league for your town

📅 EVENTS
• Festivals, funerals, weddings, church and town meetings
• RSVP, add to your calendar, and share photos in each event's album

🛒 MARKETPLACE AND OPPORTUNITIES
• Buy and sell in the community, contact sellers by phone or WhatsApp
• Find local businesses, jobs, scholarships and training

🗣️ CULTURE AND COMMUNITY
• Siwu word of the day with pronunciation
• Birthdays, "I'm home" visits for those coming back from abroad, and new member welcomes

🔒 SAFE AND RESPECTFUL
• Community rules, reporting and moderators for every town
• Block anyone, hide when you're active, and delete your account at any time
• Private messages can only be read by the people in the chat
• No ads and no selling of your data

🔔 NOTIFICATIONS
• Messages, friend requests, comments, announcements and live goals from your town's matches
• Event reminders and friends' birthdays — choose exactly which ones you want

Siwu Connect is built for slow connections and saves your data, with a light video version for 2G/3G.
